Everything you need on the road

From currency conversion to expense tracking — MyRate has you covered.

Instant Converter

Type any amount and get the result immediately. Switch direction with one tap — foreign to home or home to foreign.

Expense Log

Save expenses in one tap after a conversion. Add category, note, GPS location, and photo. Filter, summarize, and export to CSV.

Trip Budget Tracker

Create a trip with a name, destination, dates, and budget. Track daily spending against your plan and see how much is left.

Rate Calculator

Enter how much you handed over and how much you got back. MyRate calculates the real rate you received — and shows the difference in percent.

Expense Map

See all your GPS-tagged expenses on a map. Tap a pin to view the amount, category, and note for that location.

Home Screen Widget

See your active exchange rates and last conversion result right on your home screen. Tap to open the converter or log an expense directly.

*MyRate: Travel Currency Converter & Expense Tracker*

## 1. Who we are

MyRate is operated by LP Wave (the "controller", "we"). Contact: [support@lpwave.hu](mailto:support@lpwave.hu). Full provider details are in the [legal notice](/legal/).

> In short: you need an account to use MyRate. Most of what you enter stays on your device. It is stored on our servers only if you use cloud sync (subscribers) or group trips. We have no ads, no analytics SDK and we never sell your data.

## 2. What we process, and why

**Account.** Email address and password (or your Google / Apple sign-in), display name, optional profile picture, and your plan (Free, Lifetime, Subscription). Needed to provide your account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

**Your travel data.** Exchange rates, expenses (amount, currency, date, category, note), trips and budgets, settings. Optionally a **GPS location** (only when you enable it, and only at the moment you save an expense) and **photos** you attach to an expense. By default this data lives only on your device.

**Cloud sync (subscribers only).** If you have an active subscription, your trips, expenses, exchange rates, preferences, photos and trip cover images are synchronized to our server so you can use them on several devices. Without a subscription nothing is synchronized.

**Group trips.** When you create or join a group trip, the group's data is shared with its members: names, expenses and how they are split, settlements, guests, location of group expenses (if recorded), an activity log and in-app notifications. Other members can see what you enter in the group. Organizers approve join requests. You can report content and block users; reports are stored so we can review them (Art. 6(1)(f), our interest in a safe service).

**Purchases.** Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see card details. We receive verification data (store, product, transaction reference, status, expiry date) to unlock your plan (Art. 6(1)(b)).

**Feedback.** If you use the feedback form in Settings, what you type (and an optional email) is submitted through a Google Form, together with your app version and device model.

## 3. Services we use

| Service | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase (database, authentication, file storage), project hosted in London, UK | Account and the data described in section 2 | Hosting our back end |
| Apple / Google | Sign-in, in-app purchases, the device's built-in address lookup for place names (coordinates are resolved by your operating system) | Sign-in, payments, place names |
| FreeCurrencyAPI, Frankfurter, Open Exchange Rates | Currency codes (e.g. EUR, HUF) and your IP address | Exchange rates |
| OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap | Map tile requests (your IP address and the map area you view) | Showing maps |
| Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) | The place name or address you type into the map search, and your IP address | Searching places |
| Google Forms | The feedback you submit | Feedback |

These providers process data under their own terms. The UK has an EU adequacy decision, so hosting in London is covered by it.

## 4. What we do not do

- No advertising, and no sharing of data with ad networks
- No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
- No tracking of your location in the background
- No selling of personal data

## 5. How long we keep data

Account and cloud data are kept until you delete your account. Data only on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. Purchase records at Apple and Google are governed by their own policies.

## 6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app. This removes your profile, trips, expenses, exchange rates, notifications and purchase records from our servers. If you are in group trips, ownership is handed to another active member where possible; if there is none, the group is removed. Your expenses in groups you leave are kept under an anonymous placeholder participant, so that other members' settlements still add up; they are no longer linked to your identity.

## 7. Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Contact us at the address above. You may also complain to the Hungarian data protection authority (NAIH, [www.naih.hu](https://www.naih.hu)) or to the authority in your country.

## 8. Children

MyRate is not intended for children under 16.

## 9. Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.

> **This action is permanent.** Once your account is deleted, all your data will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

We're sorry to see you go. Please review the information below before requesting account deletion.

## Data that will be deleted

- Your profile and account information
- All tasks and chore history
- Calendar events and schedules
- Financial records and budgets
- Family connections and shared data
- All app settings and preferences

## Delete in the app

Settings → Account → Delete Account

## Or request deletion by email

1. Send a deletion request by email.
2. Include your **registered email address** in the request.
3. We'll verify your identity and process your request within **30 days**.
4. You'll receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

[Request account deletion](mailto:support@lpwave.hu?subject=Zumi%20Account%20Deletion%20Request)

Questions? Contact us at [support@lpwave.hu](mailto:support@lpwave.hu).

*Zumi: Family Manager*

## 1. Introduction

LP Wave ("we," "our," or "us") operates Zumi: Family Manager. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our application.

By using Zumi, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

**Information you provide:**

- **Account information:** email address and password when you create an account
- **Profile data:** name, family members, and preferences you set up
- **App content:** tasks, calendar events, financial records, and notes you create

**Automatically collected:**

- **Device information:** device type, operating system version
- **Usage data:** app interactions and feature usage (anonymized)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- To provide and maintain the Zumi service
- To sync your data across devices
- To send notifications you've enabled
- To improve and optimize the app experience
- To respond to your support requests

## 4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties.

We may share data only with:

- **Service providers:** Supabase (cloud hosting) under strict data protection agreements
- **Family members:** data you choose to share within your family group
- **Legal requirements:** when required by law

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and at rest. Your data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure with regular backups.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access:** view all your data within the app
- **Correct:** update your information anytime
- **Export:** request a copy of your data
- **Delete:** request deletion of your account and all associated data

Want to delete your account and all data? See [Delete Account](/zumi/delete-account/).

## 7. Children's Privacy

Zumi is designed for families and may be used by children under parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by updating the "Last updated" date.
